Trade marketing is the set of strategies aimed at growing sales where the
product meets the shopper — at the point of sale. No matter how strong
digital channels become, in many categories the decision is made right in
front of the shelf.

## 1. The battle for the shelf

A shopper's eyes land first on shelves at eye level. So the key task is to
place the product in the right spot, in the right form and in sufficient
quantity. A planogram takes this process out of the realm of chance.

## 2. POSM and visual communication

Wobblers, shelf-talkers, stands and other POSM materials draw the shopper's
attention and make the decision easier. The key is not to overdo it and to
keep everything on-brand.

## 3. Promo mechanics

"2+1", a discount, a gift or a loyalty promo — each mechanic should serve a
specific goal: prompt a trial, raise the average check or drive repeat
purchase. ROI is calculated after every campaign.

## 4. Execution control

Even the best strategy delivers nothing if it is executed poorly at the
point of sale. Monitoring merchandisers' work, photo reports and regular
audits keep the execution level high.

## Conclusion

Trade marketing is a system: when the right placement, clear mechanics and
strict execution control come together, sales grow steadily.
